## Benchmark Methodology

```
Tipo            | Metodo                    | Sample Size | Frequencia
----------------|---------------------------|-------------|------------
Summative       | Usability test moderado   | 8-12 users  | Trimestral
Formative       | Rapid testing             | 5 users     | Por sprint
Competitive     | Benchmark vs competitors  | 15 users    | Semestral
Unmoderated     | Remote unmoderated test   | 30+ users   | Mensal
```
