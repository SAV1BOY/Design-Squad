# Phase: Governance Model

## Objective

Estabelecer processos de governanca para contribuicao, review, aprovacao e manutencao do DS.

## Inputs

- Roadmap de componentes (02-component-roadmap.md)
- Stakeholder map
- Modelos de governanca de DS publicos

## Activities

1.

Contribution Process: definir como times propoem e contribuem componentes.

2.

Review Process: definir criterios de review (design, code, a11y, perf).

3.

Quality Gates: definir checks automatizados e manuais.

4.

Roles: definir papeis (maintainer, contributor, consumer).

5.

Communication: definir canais e cadencias (office hours, newsletter, Slack).

6.

Decision Framework: como decisoes sao tomadas e documentadas.

## Output

- Governance document publicado
- Contribution guide
- Quality gate checklist
- Communication plan
- RACI matrix
